# Telugu Hindu Wedding — Cultural Knowledge Vault

## Identity and Planning
- Confirm the families' regional Telugu customs and engage a Telugu-speaking Purohit
- Consult the Purohit or astrologer for horoscope matching, lagna, and the auspicious muhurtham
- Ask both families which rituals are essential because Andhra and Telangana practices can differ

## Telugu Pre-Wedding Customs
- Nischitartham: formal engagement, blessings, gifts, and confirmation of the wedding
- Pellikuthuru and Pellikoduku: apply turmeric and oil to the bride and groom in their respective homes
- Mangala Snanam: ceremonial auspicious bath on or before the wedding day
- Snathakam: groom's purification and readiness ceremony where observed
- Kashi Yatra: groom symbolically chooses an ascetic life before being invited into marriage

## Telugu Wedding-Day Customs
- Edurukolu: bride's family formally welcomes the groom and his procession
- Gauri Puja and Ganesh Puja: separate prayers by the bride and groom before the main ceremony
- Terasala: keep a curtain between the couple before the auspicious union ritual
- Jeelakarra Bellam: couple places cumin-and-jaggery paste on each other's head at the muhurtham, symbolising an inseparable bond
- Madhuparkam: couple changes into traditional white attire with red borders where observed
- Mangalasutra Dharana: groom ties the mangalasutram, traditionally with the agreed number of knots
- Talambralu: couple showers each other with turmeric-coloured rice in a playful blessing ritual
- Saptapadi: couple takes seven steps and vows together
- Arundhati Nakshatram and Appagintalu: view the symbolic star and complete the bride's ceremonial farewell where observed

## Telugu-Specific Bookings and Items
- Book a Telugu-speaking Purohit and document the sequence and exact muhurtham cues
- Prepare jeelakarra-bellam paste, talambralu rice, terasala curtain, mangalasutram, garlands, coconuts, betel leaves, and homam materials
- Arrange Pellikuthuru and Pellikoduku turmeric supplies and family participation
- Source suitable Telugu bridal sarees and Madhuparkam attire if the family observes that custom
- Brief the photographer and videographer on Jeelakarra Bellam, Mangalasutra Dharana, and Talambralu moments

## Fusion Guidance
- When paired with another Indian tradition, label Telugu rituals explicitly and preserve Telugu-specific tasks rather than replacing them with generic Hindu or Tamil customs
