# Shared Malaysian Wedding Knowledge (All Cultures)

This file applies to ALL Malaysian weddings regardless of culture or religion.
Always include relevant items from this file in any checklist or budget generated.

## Monsoon Season Warning (Important for Outdoor Weddings)

- **East Coast Peninsula (Pahang, Terengganu, Kelantan, Johor coast)**: Monsoon from **November to March** — heavy rain, possible flooding
- **West Coast Peninsula (KL, Selangor, Penang, Perak)**: Afternoon thunderstorms almost daily **April to October**; wettest months May–June
- **Sabah**: Northeast monsoon **October to February**
- **Sarawak**: Southwest monsoon **May to October**

> Always have a **wet weather backup plan** (tent, indoor alternative) for outdoor weddings.
> Recommend tent rentals include sidewalls (walls that can be dropped during rain).

## Documents Every Malaysian Couple Needs

- MyKad (IC) — both parties
- Birth certificates
- Single status declaration (Surat Akuan Bujang) from Commissioner of Oaths
- For non-citizens: passport + visa + relevant Embassy documentation
- If previously married: divorce certificate or death certificate of previous spouse
- Sijil Perkahwinan (marriage certificate) — issued after the wedding

## Door Gifts / Wedding Favours

Common across all Malaysian cultures:
- **Bunga telur (decorated hard-boiled eggs)** — Malay/universal
- **Chocolates** in custom packaging
- **Cookies** (kuih)
- **Mini hand sanitisers** (post-COVID trend)
- **Seed packets** (garden wedding)
- **Custom candles**
- **Personalised keychains / magnets**
- Budget: RM2–RM15 per guest

## Photography Packages — What to Expect

Standard Malaysian wedding photography package includes:
- **Pre-wedding (engagement) shoot** — outdoor location, 2–4 hours
- **Actual day coverage** — 8–12 hours
- **Edited photos** — 300–600 high-res edited images
- **Online gallery** delivery within 4–8 weeks
- **Prints / album** — additional cost (RM800–RM5,000+)

Videography package typically includes:
- Actual day coverage (same timeline as photographer)
- **Highlight reel** (3–7 minutes) — delivered 4–12 weeks post-wedding
- **Full ceremony film** (1–4 hours)
- **Same Day Edit (SDE)** — shown at the dinner reception; additional cost RM2,000–RM5,000

## Taxes & Service Charges

- **Hotel venues** charge **10% service charge + 6% SST** on top of quoted prices — factor this into budget
- Restaurant venues: similar; always ask if pricing is "nett" or "+ charges"
- Freelance vendors (florists, MUAs, photographers): usually quoted nett (all-in)
- Caterers: usually nett but confirm if setup/teardown is included

## Practical Day-Of Tips

- **Print a vendor contact list** and give it to your coordinator / best man / maid of honour
- **Confirm all vendors 1 week before** the wedding date
- **Buffer 30–60 minutes** into all timelines — Malaysian weddings always run slightly late
- **Assign someone to manage the ang pow / bunga telur table**
- **Charge all devices** the night before (photographer, DJ, photo booth)
- **Prepare an emergency kit**: safety pins, double-sided tape, stain remover pen, pain relief, snacks for the couple (you won't eat at your own wedding)
- **Send detailed directions** to venue — GPS sometimes fails for hotel entrances / dewan locations
