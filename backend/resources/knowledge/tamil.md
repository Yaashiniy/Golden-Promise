# Tamil Hindu Wedding — Cultural Knowledge Vault

## Identity and Planning
- Confirm the Tamil family customs and engage a Tamil Hindu priest (Vadhyar)
- Consult the priest or Jothidar for jathagam matching and the muhurtham date and time
- Plan the ceremony sequence around the exact muhurtham window

## Tamil Pre-Wedding Customs
- Nichayathartham: formal engagement and exchange of gifts, fruit, betel leaves, attire, and jewellery
- Sumangali Prarthanai: seek blessings from married women and honour female ancestors where observed
- Paalikai Thelippu: prepare and sprout nine grains in decorated clay pots where observed
- Nalangu: turmeric, oil, blessings, songs, and playful family rituals for the couple
- Janavasam or Maapillai Azhaippu: welcome the groom and his family where observed

## Tamil Wedding-Day Customs
- Vratham: priest-led purification and sacred-thread preparations
- Kaashi Yathirai: groom symbolically leaves for a spiritual life before the bride's father invites him to marry
- Oonjal: couple sits on a decorated swing while married women sing and perform protective rituals
- Maalai Maatral: exchange flower garlands, commonly three times
- Kanyadaanam: bride's parents formally entrust her to the groom
- Mangalya Dharanam: groom ties the thali at the precise muhurtham, accompanied by nadaswaram and getti melam
- Sapthapadi: couple takes seven steps and vows together
- Ammi Mithithal and Arundhati Paarthal: bride steps on a grindstone and the couple views the symbolic star where observed
- Nalangu after the ceremony: playful games and blessings welcoming married life

## Tamil-Specific Bookings and Items
- Book a Tamil-speaking Vadhyar and confirm the full ritual list in writing
- Book nadaswaram and tavil musicians; cue getti melam for the thali ceremony
- Order the thali, koorai pudavai, veshti, angavastram, garlands, coconuts, betel leaves, turmeric, kumkum, and homam materials
- Arrange seer varisai trays and gifts according to both families' expectations
- Arrange a banana-leaf vegetarian virundhu or agreed reception menu

## Fusion Guidance
- When paired with another Indian tradition, label Tamil rituals explicitly and do not present general Hindu customs as a substitute for Tamil-specific tasks
